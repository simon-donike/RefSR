Trainer Loop
------------
pl.Trainer from train.py orchestrates:
1. Calls datamodule for loaders → batches.
2. Executes PanS2System.train/val steps.
3. Backprop + optimizer (Adam/AdamW) and optional scheduler.
4. Logs metrics configured in cfg.trainer.
